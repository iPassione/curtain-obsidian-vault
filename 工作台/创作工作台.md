## 孤立页面检查

> [!question] 为什么保留这张表
> 没有入链的设定很容易从长期创作记忆中消失。MOC、模板和本工作台自身不计入异常。

```dataview
TABLE file.folder as "区域", join(file.tags, ", ") as "标签"
FROM ""
WHERE length(file.inlinks) = 0
AND !contains(file.tags, "moc")
AND !contains(file.path, "模板/")
SORT file.path ASC
```
